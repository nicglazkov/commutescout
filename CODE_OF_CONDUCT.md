# Code of conduct

CommuteScout follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in this repository, the related CommuteScout repositories, and
any other space where someone represents the project.

## The short version

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including in disagreement.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or unwelcome attention,
  in public or in private.
- Never publish someone else's private information, including locations
  from a trip, a watch area or a saved place.

## Reporting

Report a problem through the
[contact page](https://commutescout.com/contact). Reports are handled
privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their
consent. Maintainers can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and can
block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
