Never miss a closure on your commute

Draw an area anywhere CommuteScout covers, once. CommuteScout checks it every few minutes and sends an alert the moment an incident, closure, chain control, or wildfire shows up inside it, so you find out before you are already stuck in it.

  • Draw it. A circle around home or work, a polygon around a region, or a route with a buffer along your commute.
  • Pick what matters. Incidents, closures, chain controls, wildfires: choose any combination.
  • Get notified. Push notification, email, or both, the moment something changes.
