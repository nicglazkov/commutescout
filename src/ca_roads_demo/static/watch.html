Alerts for the roads you drive

Draw an area once, anywhere CommuteScout covers: around home, along your commute or over a mountain pass. CommuteScout checks it about every 5 minutes and tells you when an incident, closure, chain control, wildfire or weather warning shows up inside it.

  1. 01
    Draw it A circle, a shape of your own, or a route with stops along the way.
  2. 02
    Pick what matters Any mix of the five kinds, or only the report types you care about, such as crashes or full closures.
  3. 03
    Get the alert By push notification, email or both, at the next check after something changes.

What you can watch

Three shapes, any of the five kinds of alert, sent the way you choose.

  • Circle

    Around home, work or a school, up to 25 mi (40 km) out from the center.

  • Your own shape

    3 to 20 corners around a valley, a pass or a neighborhood, up to 2,000 sq mi.

  • Route

    Your drive with up to 8 stops, start and end included, up to 250 mi long, watched up to 2 mi either side.

Notifications on this device

Push alerts arrive on each browser or phone you turn them on for.

On iPhone or iPad, add this page to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen), open it from there, then turn on notifications.

Account

Deleting your account removes it at once, with every watch area, alert record and registered device. This can't be undone.

Alerts are informational only and can be late, incomplete or wrong. Check your state's 511 before you drive, and never rely on CommuteScout for evacuation or emergency decisions. We store your account email and the areas you draw to run your alerts: see the privacy page and the terms.
