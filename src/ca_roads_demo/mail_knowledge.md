### The product

- CommuteScout shows live road conditions from official sources: crashes,
  closures, lane closures, chain controls, cameras, signs, road weather
  and wildfires. What each state publishes differs; the data sources page
  lists it.
- It is free while in beta. There is no paid plan to buy yet.
- CommuteScout Drive is the phone app: turn-by-turn navigation that reads
  out what is ahead on the route. Android installs from an APK on GitHub
  (it is not on Google Play). iPhone installs through TestFlight.
- One account works on the web and both phones. Sign in with Google or
  Apple, or with an email link on the web.
- New accounts for watch areas are approved by hand, so a first sign-in
  can show a "waiting for approval" message. Nic gets a notice and
  approves it, usually within a day. You cannot approve accounts and
  cannot see who is approved: use the "draft" lane for these.
- It is not affiliated with any government agency. Always check the
  state's own 511 or DOT site before driving.

### Known issues and answers

- Android, Google sign-in fails with "[28444] Developer console is not set
  up correctly": this was a bug on our side, fixed in version 0.6.2.
  Install the newest APK over the old one and sign in again.
- Android updates do not install themselves. Download the newest APK from
  the releases page and install it over the old version; nothing is lost.
- Android says the app is from an unknown source: that is expected for an
  APK from outside Google Play. Allow the install when asked.
- iPhone updates arrive through TestFlight. A new beta build can take a
  day or two to appear for public testers after it is released on
  Android, because Apple reviews beta builds first.
- Data missing or stale for one state: some agencies' feeds go down or
  block us from time to time. The status section says which sources are
  failing right now, if any. Do not guess when one will be back.
- Push alerts for watch areas need notifications allowed for the app (or
  for the site in the browser) and at least one watch area drawn at
  commutescout.com/watch.
- Deleting an account: the person can do it themselves from the watch
  page while signed in.
